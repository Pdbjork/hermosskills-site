Mini teardown template

  1. Surface: one public page, checkout, waitlist, catalog, or support path.
  2. Change: the exact CTA, copy, route, metric, or follow-up asset the operator would touch first.
  3. Proof: current baseline, live URL or file path, and one check that says whether the work moved anything.
  4. Approval gate: what Pete can prepare alone, and what waits for your yes before it leaves your account.
